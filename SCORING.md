# SCORING.md

Score each weekly report from **1–5** on the following:

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Importance of selected developments | 25% |
| Source quality & verification | 25% |
| Depth of analysis | 20% |
| Signal-to-noise | 15% |
| Korea relevance | 10% |
| Clarity & structure | 5% |

## Score

`Weighted Score = Σ(score × weight)`

Target: **4.0 / 5.0 or higher**

## Improvement Rule

After each report:

1. Identify the **lowest-scoring criterion**.
2. Record **one specific reason** it scored poorly.
3. Record **one concrete change** for the next report.
4. Do not add new rules unless the same weakness appears repeatedly.

The goal is continuous improvement without making the process unnecessarily complex.
